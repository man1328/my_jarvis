--- RECIPE: BREAD ---

Here's a simple recipe for classic white bread:

Ingredients:

* 2 cups of all-purpose flour
* 1 teaspoon of salt
* 1 teaspoon of sugar
* 1 packet (2 1/4 teaspoons) of active dry yeast
* 1 cup of warm water (around 100°F to 110°F)

Instructions:

1. In a large mixing bowl, combine flour, salt, sugar, and yeast.
2. Add warm water to the dry ingredients and mix until a shaggy dough forms.
3. Knead the dough on a floured surface for 10 minutes, until it becomes smooth and elastic.
4. Place the dough in a greased bowl, cover it with plastic wrap, and let it rise in a warm place for 1 hour, or until it has doubled in size.
5. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
6. Punch down the dough and shape it into a loaf.
7. Place the loaf in a greased 9x5-inch loaf pan and bake for 30-35 minutes, or until the bread is golden brown and sounds hollow when tapped.
8. Let the bread cool on a wire rack for at least 10 minutes before slicing and serving.
