--- RECIPE: SOURDOUGH ---

Here's a classic sourdough recipe:

**Ingredients:**

* 1 cup warm water (around 90°F to 100°F)
* 1/4 cup active sourdough starter (100% hydration, see notes)
* 3 cups bread flour
* 1 tsp salt

**Instructions:**

1. In a small bowl, combine warm water and sourdough starter. Stir gently to dissolve the starter.
2. In a large mixing bowl, combine flour and salt.
3. Gradually add the sourdough starter mixture to the dry ingredients. Mix until just combined.
4. Knead the dough for 10 minutes using a stand mixer or by hand.
5. Place the dough in a lightly oiled bowl, cover with plastic wrap or a damp towel, and let rise at room temperature for 4-6 hours, or until doubled in size.
6. Preheat oven to 450°F (230°C) with a Dutch oven or heavy-bottomed pot with a lid inside (optional).
7. Punch down the dough and shape into a round or oblong loaf.
8. Place the dough onto a baking sheet or peel, if using.
9. Score the bread with a sharp knife or razor blade, about 1/4 inch deep.
10. Bake for 25-35 minutes, or until the bread is golden brown and sounds hollow when tapped on the bottom.
11. Remove from oven and let cool on a wire rack for at least 1 hour before slicing.

**Notes:**

* For a more intense sourdough flavor, use a 100% hydration starter (fed with only water).
* If you don't have an active sourdough starter, create one by mixing 1/2 cup flour and 1/2 cup water. Let it sit at room temperature for 24-48 hours before using.
* Adjust the proofing time based on your environment and preferance for crust color.
